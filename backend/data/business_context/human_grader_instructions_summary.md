# Business Plan Competition Grading Guidelines & Standards

## **Overall Competition Purpose & Judging Philosophy**
*   **Primary Goal:** Award prize money to businesses with the highest potential for **long-term success** to boost local economies and promote self-reliance.
*   **Core Evaluation Focus:** Judge the **quality and viability of the business plan** over presentation style or production quality.
*   **Key Mindset:** Be objective and mindful of biases. Consider local economic conditions, cultural practices, and market-specific factors. Do not penalize language barriers or presentation nervousness.

---

## **Judging Structure & Point Allocation**

### **Semi-Finals Evaluation**
*   **Business Plan (Slide Deck & Content): 80%** of total score.
*   **Video Presentation: 20%** of total score.

### **Finals Evaluation**
*   **Business Plan (Slide Deck & Content): 80%** of total score.
*   **Live Q&A Performance: 20%** of total score.
    *   Teams must demonstrate a deep understanding of their business, its challenges, and opportunities.

---

## **Business Plan Grading Criteria (80% of Score)**
*This is the primary focus. Plans are evaluated via the submitted Google Slides deck.*

**1. Problem & Customer Pain Point (Weight: High)**
*   **Good Plan:** Clearly defines a specific problem with supporting data validating its existence and market need.
*   **Bad Plan:** Vaguely describes a problem without evidence or confuses a broad societal issue with a specific, addressable customer pain point.

**2. Business Venture, Product, or Service (Weight: High)**
*   **Good Plan:** Explains *how* the product/service efficiently and cost-effectively solves the defined problem. Details cost structure, value proposition, and revenue model.
*   **Bad Plan:** Describes the product/service without clearly linking it to problem resolution. Lacks details on costs or how money is made.

**3. Market Size & Growth Analysis (Weight: High)**
*   **Good Plan:** Analyzes the **local/target market** (not country or world). Identifies competitors' strengths/weaknesses. Demonstrates realistic expansion plans and risk mitigation strategies.
*   **Bad Plan:** Defines market too broadly (e.g., "all of Nigeria"). Fails to identify competitors or differentiators. Has no discussion of risks or growth plans.

**4. Marketing & Sales Strategy (Weight: Medium)**
*   **Good Plan:** Outlines clear strategies for customer acquisition, pricing, promotion, and distribution.
*   **Bad Plan:** Strategy is absent, generic, or not tailored to the local market and customer base.

**5. Financials (Weight: Critical)**
*   **Good Plan:** Provides detailed, realistic financial data.
    *   **For existing businesses:** Month-by-month breakdown of revenue/expenses for the past year. Basic profit/expense/revenue for past 3 years (if available).
    *   **For all:** Financial forecasts (sales projections, expenses) with identified risk factors. Financials should be grounded in past performance or well-researched assumptions.
*   **Bad Plan:** Provides only aspirational, round-number projections without basis. Lacks historical data (if business exists) or detailed breakdowns. Financials appear invented.

**6. Management Team (Weight: Medium)**
*   **Good Plan:** Highlights relevant team expertise, experience, and adaptability.
*   **Bad Plan:** Team section is missing, weak, or lacks relevant skills for the venture.

**7. Use of Prize Money (Weight: Medium)**
*   **Good Plan:** Clearly lists specific, budgeted uses for the prize money and explains how each item will contribute concretely to business growth. Justifies the business's profitability.
*   **Bad Plan:** Vague description of fund use (e.g., "for expansion"). No cost breakdown or clear link to growth.

## **Video Presentation Grading (20% in Semi-Finals)**
*   **Format Compliance is Mandatory:** Points will be deducted or entry disqualified for violations.
    *   **Ideal Length:** ~5 minutes. **Point Deduction:** For videos under 4 minutes or over 5 minutes 30 seconds.
    *   **Required Visuals:** Participant's face **and** presentation slides must be visible throughout.
    *   **Content Priority:** Focus on clarity of business model over high production quality. Including pictures of the business, employees, and customers is valued.
*   **Scoring Principle:** Judge the clarity and effectiveness of communicating the business plan, not video editing skills.

---

## **Critical Requirements & Automatic Disqualifiers**
Failure to comply results in elimination or zero points for that section.

### **Submission Format (Absolute Requirement)**
*   Slide deck must be submitted via **Google Slides**.
*   Video must be submitted via **YouTube** (full video, not Shorts).
*   Participant's face must be visible alongside slides in the video.

### **Prohibited Business Types**
Plans for the following are excluded and must be disqualified:
*   Multi-level/network marketing, pyramid schemes, get-rich-quick programs.
*   Buyouts, real-estate syndications, tax shelters.
*   Franchises or regional licensing agreements.

### **AI Usage Policy**
*   **Permitted:** Using AI for grammar/spelling checks, clarity editing, or research.
*   **Grounds for Disqualification:** Using AI to generate the business plan content, create slides, or make the video presentation. Judges must flag suspected AI-generated content.

### **Required Deliverables (Missing items may cause point loss or disqualification)**
*   Business License/Proof of Registration (matching business name/type).
*   Proof of Business Bank Account (sensitive info covered).
*   Pictures of business/product/workspace/employees in the slide deck.

---

## **Feedback Guidelines for Judges**
*Feedback is required and will be sent directly to applicants.*

**Feedback Must Be:**
*   **Thorough & Specific:** Avoid one-liners. Address both strengths and areas for improvement.
*   **Professional & Considerate:** Written directly to the business team in a supportive tone.
*   **Actionable:** Provide ideas that could boost their business plan.
*   **Clear & Jargon-Free:** Avoid business acronyms and complex terms. Use simple English.

**Key Questions to Guide Feedback:**
1.  Why was the score high or low in each section?
2.  What is one idea to improve their business plan?
3.  What did you want to see more or less of?
4.  Which aspect of their plan, if emphasized, would lead to the greatest success?

**Examples of Helpful vs. Unhelpful Feedback:**
*   **Helpful:** "Your financial records are well-organized. To strengthen your plan, narrow your target market from 'all Nigerians' to a specific geographic or demographic group you can reach effectively. Also, please describe your main competitors and what makes your service different."
*   **Unhelpful:** "Good job!" or "No local market info." or "Your ROI doesn’t make sense." (Too vague, negative without direction, or uses jargon).
